[page 6] HALE ALOHA CHURCH — This sheet documents Hale Aloha Church in Lahaina, Maui, Hawaii, as part of the Historic American Buildings Survey. It features a regional map of the Island of Maui and a detailed site plan of Lahaina showing street layouts and adjacent structures. The sheet includes extensive historical text describing the church's construction from coral stone in 1855 and its subsequent use and decline. Credits acknowledge the National Park Service, Lahaiana Restoration Foundation, and student architects from Texas A&M, University of Hawaii, and Kansas State University.

[page 8] HALE ALOHA CHURCH — The sheet displays architectural documentation for Hale Aloha Church, featuring a 'Door Detail', a 'Southwest Elevation', and a 'Northeast Elevation'. The drawings illustrate a long, single-story structure with a gable roof, clerestory windows, and multiple entryways. Notable construction details indicated include cement plaster walls, a lava rock rubble foundation, cut coral quoining, and wood shingle roofing.
[page 9] HALE ALOHA CHURCH — This sheet presents architectural drawings for the Hale Aloha Church located in Lahaina, Maui County, Hawaii. It displays the Northwest and Southeast elevations of the structure, highlighting its gabled roof, central tower/chimney, and fenestration. Notable architectural features include wood shingle siding, cement plaster over lava rock rubble walls, and detailed sections for window heads, jambs, sills, and door thresholds. The drawings document construction materials such as wood lintels and concrete, and were produced in 1966 for the Historic American Buildings Survey.
[page 10] HALE ALOHA CHURCH — This sheet contains architectural details and a longitudinal section for the Hale Aloha Church in Lahaina, Maui. The upper portion features detailed drawings of a lamp bracket, front and back platform trims/moulds, and window and door head trim. The primary drawing, Section AA, illustrates the building's structural cross-section, noting rafters at 3'-4" o.c., a louvered window, wood lintels over openings, and walls constructed of smooth finish plaster over cut coral stone quoining and lava rubble, resting on a rock foundation.
[page 11] HALE ALOHA CHURCH — This is a longitudinal building section (Section BB) of the Hale Aloha Church in Lahaina, Maui County, Hawaii. The drawing illustrates the structural assembly of the gable roof, featuring wood shingles, rafters, and alternating 2x4 and 3x4 framing members, supported by walls of plaster over coral stone and lava rock masonry. Additional details on the right show window head, jamb, and sill configurations, along with notes on braces and eave conditions.
