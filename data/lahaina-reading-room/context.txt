[page 3] MASTERS READING ROOM — The sheet documents the Masters Reading Room in Lahaina, Maui, Hawaii, as part of the Historic American Buildings Survey. It includes a regional map of Maui, a detailed site plan of Lahaina showing street layouts and the building's location near the harbor, and descriptive text blocks outlining the site's history and the building's construction. The text notes the two-story structure was completed in 1834 as a meeting place for visiting ship officers, built with mission funds, and is currently used for living quarters.
[page 4] MASTERS READING ROOM — This sheet presents the First Floor Plan and First Floor Window Details for the Masters Reading Room in Lahaina, Maui County, Hawaii. The floor plan depicts a concrete structure with mixed masonry walls composed of coral, lava, adobe, lime, and mortar, featuring structural elements like 6x6 beams and posts. Architectural details on the right show cross-sections for window heads, jambs, and sills. The drawing was prepared by E. Davis Chauviere in 1966 for the Lahaina Restoration Foundation under the National Park Service as part of the Historic American Buildings Survey.
[page 5] MASTERS READING ROOM — This sheet displays the Second Floor Plan of the Masters Reading Room in Lahaina, Maui County, Hawaii, prepared for the Historic American Buildings Survey in 1936. The plan outlines a layout including a carport, temporary storage, and a main living area with 3/4" x 5 1/4" T&G flooring and a temporary fiberboard partition. Architectural features noted include wood railings, concrete walls, and galvanized iron roofing. To the right, detailed cross-sections labeled Head, Jamb, and Sill illustrate second-floor window construction details.
[page 6] MASTERS READING ROOM — The sheet presents four architectural elevations (Northwest, Southwest, Northeast, Southeast) of the Masters Reading Room located in Lahaina, Maui County, Hawaii. Notable features include gable roofs with G.I. roofing, cement plaster over masonry walls, mixed masonry of coral lava stone and adobe, concrete foundations, carports, wood stairs, and wood railings. The drawings document the exterior facade details, window placements, and structural materials of the historic building.
