[page 4] COURT & CUSTOM HOUSE — This sheet documents the Court & Custom House in Lahaina, Maui, Hawaii. It features a regional map of the Island of Maui and a detailed site plan of Lahaina town highlighting the building's location near Front Street and Prison Street. Descriptive text outlines the building's history from the 1850s, noting its construction as a two-story stone structure with a flat roof and verandah, serving various governmental functions. The sheet also details later repairs and reconstruction, crediting the Historic American Buildings Survey and student architects from 1966.

[page 6] FIRST FLOOR PLAN — This is a first-floor architectural plan of the Court and Custom House in Lahaina, Maui County, Hawaii. The drawing depicts the layout of various rooms including an Old Post Office, Generator Storage, Strong Room, and several Offices. Notable architectural features noted on the plan include exposed steel columns, hard wall plaster on wood lath, a metal ceiling in the strong room, and a concrete porch supported by reinforced concrete columns. The sheet was prepared by Ralston H. Nagata in 1966 for the Lahaina Restoration Foundation under the direction of the National Park Service.

Notable features include louvered and casement windows, an iron-railed balcony, folding grills, and concrete steps. Material annotations specify coral and rubble walls with a cement plaster finish.

Key details include a central columned portico entrance, casement windows on the upper story, metal bars on the ground floor openings, and a 1931 addition for generator storage built with coral and rubble walls finished in cement plaster.
